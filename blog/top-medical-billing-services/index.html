Medical Billing Wholesalers is a global provider of medical billing and coding services, often working as a back-office solution for other billing companies or directly with practices. They emphasize cost-effectiveness and efficiency through offshore operations.

Pricing Model: Per-claim (estimated $2-$5 per claim) or hourly rates for specific tasks.

  • ⚠️ Communication can sometimes be a challenge due to time zone differences.
